Day bar: four animation options

Each bar shows the day 67% done, at its real size on the TV. The animations are live.

A · Glint

Solid navy, with the same light-blue comet that runs along the day line sweeping up the bar. It matches the rest of the graph.

B · Liquid

Navy liquid in a tube: the surface ripples and a few light-blue bubbles rise. Calm, and it reads as "filling up".

C · Blocks

The bar is split into today's blocks in their own colors (like the ramps). Done blocks are solid, the live one fills and breathes, later ones are faint.

D · Glowing top

Solid navy with a bright cap at the top that pulses outward, like the "now" dot. The dashed line looks like it grows out of it.
